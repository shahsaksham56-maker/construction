The Quarry House

The Quarry House

Location

Cotswolds, UK

Year

2024

Type

New Build

Area

620 m²

Programme

19 months

Value

£4.1M

The brief

Built from the ground it stands on — stone cut from a dormant quarry 900 metres away, laid by four masons over eleven months.

Site area

1.8 ha

Built area

620 m²

Levels

2

Structure

Load-bearing stone + CLT

Envelope

Local oolitic limestone

Certification

Passivhaus Classic

The constraint

Conservation officers required 100% local stone. The nearest active quarry had a 14-month lead time.

What we did

We reopened a dormant seam under licence, set up a cutting yard on site, and trained two apprentice masons for the programme.

The result

Zero stone haulage beyond 1 km. Passivhaus certified on first airtightness test at 0.4 ACH.

The quarry

Reopening a dormant seam was cheaper than waiting fourteen months for the nearest active quarry. We took a short licence, set a cutting yard on the plot itself, and trained two apprentice masons through the programme.

Envelope

Passivhaus was never a marketing target here — it was the only way to heat a solid stone house without a plant room. The wall build-up puts insulation inboard of the stone, with a service void that keeps the airtightness layer completely unpenetrated.

  • 100% stone from 900 m away, zero haulage beyond 1 km

  • 0.4 ACH at first airtightness test

  • Two apprentices trained and certified on site
